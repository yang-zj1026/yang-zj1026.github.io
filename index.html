Hi! I'm a Research Engineer at Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL). Previously I was a Founding Research Engineer at Assured Robot Intelligence (ARI). My research focuses on Robot Learning to bring humanoid robots to real-world applications.

I've been fortunate to work with Prof. Xiaolong Wang at UCSD, and with Prof. Erdem Bıyık and Prof. Gaurav Sukhatme at USC. I received my MSCS degree from USC and my BEng degree in Computer Science from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
